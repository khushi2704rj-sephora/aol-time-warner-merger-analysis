# Contributing

We welcome contributions to this M&A case study!

## How to Contribute

1.  **Fork the repository**
2.  **Enhance the Analysis**:
    -   Add new data on the post-split performance of AOL and Time Warner.
    -   Compare this merger to other tech/media consolidations (e.g., AT&T/Time Warner).
    -   Refine the visual timeline or financial breakdown.
3.  **Open a Pull Request** with your insights.

## Standards
-   Cite authoritative sources (SEC filings, major financial news).
-   Maintain objectivity in historical analysis.
